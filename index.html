Transformation 轉化

Mental & Emotion

精神與情緒狀態

Greater alertness and motivation were noticeable after waking. Overall outlook became more optimistic, with a stronger tendency to feel satisfied with everyday life. Emotional responses also became calmer, with substantially lower levels of stress, anger, nervousness, and fear. Positive details in the surrounding environment became easier to notice. A stronger sense of control and direction in daily life also developed, while the perception of continued improvement contributed to greater self-confidence.

起床後比較有精神、有動力做事，心態轉變為更加樂觀，更容易滿足，心情變得非常平靜，不論是壓力、生氣、緊張、害怕的程度都大幅減少，並且更容易發現日常生活周遭的美好。擁有生活掌控感及目標感，能夠感受到自己正在變好而提升自信心。

Physical & Energy

生理與精力狀態

Sleepiness began to occur naturally at around 8:30 p.m. each day. Energy remained relatively stable throughout the rest of the day, with daytime drowsiness becoming uncommon. Both physical stamina and overall energy levels showed a noticeable improvement.

每天到約晚上八點半就會自動想睡覺，其餘時間都有精力，不會輕易想睡，體能、精力都有感受到提升。

Cognitive Output & Focus

專注與學習產出

Sustained attention improved noticeably, allowing longer periods of focus on a single task. Difficult tasks also became easier to initiate, substantially reducing the previous tendency to procrastinate. Earlier waking created a greater sense of available time during the day, making it easier to begin activities that required longer uninterrupted periods.

專注力明顯提升，能夠持續一段時間專注在一件事情上，並且可以輕易開始做困難的事，大幅改善之前的拖延習慣。由於早上很早起，也覺得時間變多了，能夠更容易開始做需要大量時間的事情。
